# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated: October 1, 2026**

This policy explains how MyVedicStar uses cookies and similar browser storage.

## Cookies

The MyVedicStar web application does not set its own cookies.

When you sign in, you are taken to our identity provider, Microsoft Entra External ID. It uses cookies on its own sign-in pages to keep you signed in securely and to protect against fraud. Those cookies are governed by Microsoft's policies.

## Browser storage we use

MyVedicStar stores a small amount of information in your browser so the app works the way you expect:

- **Your preferences (local storage):** your selected location, recent locations, tradition and appearance choice, and whether you declined location permission (so we do not ask again), so they are remembered on your next visit. They stay on your device until you clear them.
- **Sign-in in progress (session storage):** temporary security values used only while you are being redirected to and from the sign-in page. They are removed when sign-in completes and never contain your password.

Your sign-in tokens are kept in memory only and are cleared when you close or reload the page.

## Analytics

Analytics are not collected without your consent. If optional analytics are introduced, we will ask for your consent first where required by law.

## Your choices

You can clear cookies and browser storage at any time in your browser settings. Clearing them signs you out and resets your saved preferences.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through **Contact Us**.
