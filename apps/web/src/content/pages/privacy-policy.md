**Last Updated: October 1, 2026**

MyVedicStar respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, mobile applications, and related services (“Services”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MyVedicStar, we may collect the following information.

### Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect information such as:

- First name
- Last name
- Email address
- Address
- City
- State or province
- Postal/ZIP code
- Country

Authentication may be provided through Microsoft Entra External ID or other identity providers that we support.

We do not store your account password in the MyVedicStar application database when authentication is handled by an external identity provider.

### Location Information

Many Panchang and Muhurta calculations depend on geographic location.

You may provide location information by:

- Selecting a city
- Entering a postal/ZIP code
- Providing latitude and longitude
- Allowing your device or browser to provide your current location

Location information may be used to calculate sunrise, sunset, Panchang information, Muhurta periods, and other location-dependent results.

Where device location permission is requested, you can choose whether to grant that permission through your browser or device settings.

### Birth and Astrology Information

If you use astrology, horoscope, compatibility, or related features, we may collect information such as:

- Date of birth
- Time of birth
- Place of birth
- Birth location coordinates
- Astrology preferences
- Saved charts or profiles

This information is used to provide the features you request.

### Technical Information

When you use our Services, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

- IP address
- Browser type
- Device type
- Operating system
- Application version
- Login timestamps
- Request and diagnostic information
- Security and authentication events

We use this information for security, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, reliability, and service improvement.
