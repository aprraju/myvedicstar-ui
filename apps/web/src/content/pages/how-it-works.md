# How It Works

MyVedicStar turns traditional Panchang calculations into simple, location-aware guidance.

## 1. Choose your location

Use your current location, search for a city or ZIP code, or enter latitude and longitude. Every timing depends on where you are, because sunrise, sunset and the Panchang change from place to place. All times are shown in the local time of the selected location.

## 2. Choose your tradition

Regional traditions show different names and timings — for example, Telugu calendars show Varjyam and Durmuhurtham, and North Indian calendars show Choghadiya. Your tradition is independent of where you live.

## 3. See how today is

The day is summarised as favorable, mixed or one for caution, with:

- The good time now or next, and the next caution period
- Today's Panchang: Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Vara and Paksha
- A timeline from sunrise to the next sunrise
- Auspicious times and times to avoid

When an auspicious period overlaps a caution period, it is shown as a warning with the overlapping time, never as an unqualified good time.

## 4. Plan ahead

The calendar shows every day of the month with its Tithi, rating, festivals and observances. Select a day to see its full Panchang and periods. The festivals page lists upcoming festivals and observances for your tradition.

## How the calculations are made

Panchang elements are calculated astronomically for your location and date. Festivals and observances follow the rules of your selected tradition. Different traditions, Panchang publications or calculation methods may produce different results — see our **Disclaimer**.
