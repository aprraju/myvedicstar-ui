# Accessibility

**Last Updated: October 1, 2026**

MyVedicStar is designed to be usable by everyone. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we do

- Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a "Skip to main content" link
- Pages have a clear heading structure and landmarks for screen readers, and page changes are announced
- Colors meet contrast requirements in light and dark mode, and meaning is never conveyed by color alone — ratings, cautions and conflicts always have text and an icon
- Text resizes up to 200% without loss of content, and layouts adapt to phones, tablets, foldables and large screens
- Animations respect your "reduce motion" setting
- Forms have labels, hints and clear error messages
- Automated accessibility checks run on every change

## Known limitations

Some content, such as festival icons supplied by our calendar data, is decorative and hidden from screen readers; the festival name is always available as text.

## Feedback

If you find something difficult to use, please tell us through **Contact Us** and choose "Technical Issue". We welcome feedback and will try to respond and improve.
