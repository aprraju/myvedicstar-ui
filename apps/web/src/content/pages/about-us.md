# About Us

MyVedicStar makes the Hindu Panchang easy to understand. Beginners see simple guidance about the day; experienced users can open every detail.

## What we offer

- Today's Panchang for your location: Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Vara and Paksha
- Sunrise, sunset and a timeline of the day's auspicious and caution periods
- A month calendar with each day's Tithi, rating, festivals and observances
- Upcoming festivals and observances in your chosen regional tradition
- Clear explanations of why a day is rated as it is

## Our approach

Timings are calculated for your exact location and time zone using astronomical methods. Regional traditions — for example Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam and North Indian — can show different names and periods, and you can choose the one you follow.

The daily rating is our own summary of the five Panchanga limbs; it is not a personal Muhurta. For important ceremonies and life events, please consult a qualified priest or astrologer.
